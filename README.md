# SALES-SQL-CASESTUDY
Sales Data Analytics Case Study
Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive Sales Data Analytics Case Study that explores historical sales performance using SQL and Microsoft Excel. The goal of the analysis was to transform raw transactional sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, aggregation, trend analysis, profitability analysis, and dashboard visualisation.

The project focuses on understanding sales performance, profit trends, cost efficiency, and sales volume patterns across different months and years. The analysis demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow from querying data in SQL to building an interactive dashboard in Excel.

This project was completed as part of my data analytics portfolio to showcase my skills in SQL, Excel, data cleaning, data visualisation, and business analysis.

Business Problem

Businesses often collect large amounts of sales data but struggle to extract actionable insights from it.

Management wanted to answer questions such as:

How have sales changed over time?
Which months generated the highest revenue?
Which months were the most profitable?
How do sales compare with the cost of sales?
How has sales volume changed across the year?
Which months contribute the most to annual sales?
Is the business becoming more efficient over time?

By answering these questions, decision-makers can improve budgeting, inventory planning, pricing strategies, and overall financial performance.

SQL Analysis

The analysis was performed using SQL to aggregate sales data by year and month.

The SQL workflow included:

Grouping data by year and month
Calculating total sales
Calculating total cost of sales
Calculating total quantity sold
Calculating profit
Calculating average sales
Calculating average cost
Calculating average quantity sold
Counting the number of sales days

These aggregated results were exported to Excel for dashboard development.

Data Cleaning

Splitting CSV data into columns

Sorting months chronologically
Creating structured Excel tables
Preparing data for PivotTables and charts

Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important business insights.

Sales Trend

Monthly sales showed noticeable fluctuations throughout the year, indicating periods of stronger and weaker demand.

Profitability

Profit did not always increase at the same rate as sales, suggesting that higher revenue months were not necessarily the most efficient.

Cost Analysis

Comparing sales and cost of sales highlighted months where operational costs reduced overall profitability.

Sales Volume

Quantity sold provided additional context beyond revenue and helped identify changes in customer purchasing activity.

Profit Margin

Profit margin analysis showed the business’s ability to convert revenue into profit and highlighted months with stronger operational performance.

Revenue Contribution

Some months contributed a significantly larger share of annual revenue, indicating potential seasonal opportunities.
